# Plan 8.1 Summary: Real-Time Anomaly & Threat Detection Engine

## Executive Overview
Constructed the continuous anomaly detection and risk scoring engine for SplitLedger AI authentication and access control.

## Key Deliverables Implemented
1. **Haversine Distance & Travel Velocity (`src/lib/security/anomaly-detector.ts`)**:
   - Computes great-circle distance between consecutive login geographical coordinates.
   - Calculates speed $v = \Delta d / \Delta t$. Flags impossible travel if $v > 900 \text{ km/h}$ within $<4 \text{ hours}$ (+60 risk points).
2. **Device & Geolocation Anomaly Detection**:
   - Evaluates device fingerprints against known devices (+25 risk points).
   - Detects logins from previously unseen countries (+30 risk points).
   - Monitors failed login bursts via sliding-window tracker (+45–60 risk points).
3. **Automated Threat Mitigation & Notification Dispatcher**:
   - Classifies risk tier: `low` (<30), `medium` (30–59), `high` (60–79), `critical` (>=80).
   - Automatically records threat events in `threatMonitor` and security alerts in `security_audit_events`.
   - Dispatches in-app security notifications alerting users of suspicious access.
