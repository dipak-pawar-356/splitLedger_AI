# SplitLedger AI User Guide

## Getting Started

SplitLedger AI is an intelligent expense splitting application that helps you track shared expenses, manage contacts, and settle debts with friends, family, and colleagues.

### Sign Up

1. Visit [splitledger.ai](https://splitledger.ai)
2. Click "Sign Up" in the top right corner
3. Sign up using your email, Google, or Apple account
4. Complete your profile setup

### Dashboard Overview

The dashboard provides a quick overview of your financial activity:

- **Total Balance**: Your overall balance across all contacts
- **This Month**: Expenses and income for the current month
- **Pending Settlements**: Amount owed to you and by you
- **Recent Activity**: Latest transactions and settlements

## Contacts

### Adding a Contact

1. Navigate to **Contacts** from the sidebar
2. Click **Add Contact**
3. Fill in the contact details:
   - Name (required)
   - Email (optional)
   - Phone (optional)
   - Default currency
   - Opening balance
   - Notes
4. Click **Save**

### Managing Contacts

- **View Details**: Click on a contact to see their transaction history and balance
- **Edit**: Click the edit button to update contact information
- **Delete**: Remove a contact (this will also remove their transaction history)

### Contact Ledger

Each contact has a ledger showing:
- All transactions with that contact
- Running balance
- Payment history
- Notes and attachments

## Transactions

### Creating a Transaction

1. Navigate to **Transactions** from the sidebar
2. Click **Add Transaction**
3. Fill in the transaction details:
   - Type (paid, received, lent, borrowed, repaid)
   - Amount
   - Currency
   - Description
   - Date
   - Contact (optional)
   - Category (optional)
   - Group (optional)
   - Payment method
   - Receipt (optional)
4. Click **Create**

### Transaction Types

- **Paid**: You paid for something (e.g., you paid for dinner)
- **Received**: You received money (e.g., someone paid you back)
- **Lent**: You lent money to someone
- **Borrowed**: You borrowed money from someone
- **Repaid**: You repaid a debt

### Managing Transactions

- **View Details**: Click on a transaction to see full details
- **Edit**: Modify transaction details
- **Delete**: Remove a transaction
- **Add Receipt**: Upload a receipt image

### Receipts

Upload receipts for transactions by:
1. Clicking on a transaction
2. Clicking **Add Receipt**
3. Uploading an image or file
4. The AI will automatically extract amount, date, and merchant

## Groups

### Creating a Group

1. Navigate to **Groups** from the sidebar
2. Click **Create Group**
3. Fill in group details:
   - Name
   - Description
   - Type (trip, home, office, friends, family, other)
   - Default currency
4. Click **Create**

### Adding Members

1. Open a group
2. Click **Add Member**
3. Select from existing contacts or create a new contact
4. Click **Add**

### Group Expenses

1. Open a group
2. Click **Add Expense**
3. Fill in expense details:
   - Amount
   - Description
   - Who paid
   - Split type (equal, custom, percentage)
   - Split details
4. Click **Create**

### Split Types

- **Equal**: Split equally among all members
- **Custom**: Specify exact amounts for each member
- **Percentage**: Split by percentage

### Group Balances

View balances for each group member:
- Positive balance: Member is owed money
- Negative balance: Member owes money
- Zero balance: Member is settled

## Settlements

### Viewing Settlements

1. Navigate to **Settlements** from the sidebar
2. View all pending and completed settlements
3. Filter by status, date, or group

### Creating a Settlement

1. Click **Add Settlement**
2. Fill in settlement details:
   - From (who is paying)
   - To (who is receiving)
   - Amount
   - Payment method
   - Notes
3. Click **Create**

### Settlement Status

- **Pending**: Settlement not yet completed
- **Completed**: Settlement has been paid
- **Cancelled**: Settlement was cancelled

### Optimizing Settlements

Use the **Optimize Settlements** feature to:
- Minimize the number of transactions needed
- Calculate the most efficient way to settle debts
- Save time and reduce transaction fees

## Reports

### Generating Reports

1. Navigate to **Reports** from the sidebar
2. Select a time period (this week, this month, this year, custom)
3. Filter by category, contact, or group
4. Click **Generate Report**

### Report Types

- **Expense by Category**: Breakdown of spending by category
- **Expense by Contact**: Spending with each contact
- **Expense by Group**: Group expense breakdown
- **Monthly Trends**: Monthly spending over time

### Exporting Reports

Export reports in various formats:
- **CSV**: For spreadsheet applications
- **PDF**: For printing or sharing
- **JSON**: For developers

### Printing Reports

Click **Print Report** to generate a printable version.

## Troubleshooting

### Transaction Not Showing

- Refresh the page
- Check if transaction is in the correct date range
- Verify you're on the correct account

### Balance Incorrect

- Check all transactions for errors
- Verify settlement status
- Recalculate group balances

### Notifications Not Working

- Check notification settings
- Verify email/phone is correct
- Check spam folder for email notifications

### App Not Loading

- Check internet connection
- Clear browser cache
- Try a different browser
- Contact support if issue persists
